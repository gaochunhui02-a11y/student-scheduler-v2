How it works

    Add your courses and deadlines

  2. 2

Planning that explains itself

Recovery, not guilt

When life happens, “Catch up” finds free time before each deadline and shows its reasoning in plain words — “Due Thursday with 3 h of work left. Wednesday has 2 h free, so it starts there.”

  • Locked sessions and fixed events never move
  • Warns you honestly when there isn't enough time

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The free plan is a complete planner, and during the beta every feature is free. If Pro launches, free accounts keep everything listed under Free.

Does it use AI?

No. Planning, catch-up and syllabus import are deterministic rules you can inspect — the same input always gives the same plan, and every suggestion explains its reasons. Nothing you type is sent to an AI service.

Will it guarantee better grades?

No app can. It helps you see what's due, spread the work realistically, and recover when you fall behind — the studying is still yours.

What happens to my data?

It's stored in your own account, protected so only you can read it. No ads, no selling data, no third-party trackers. Anonymous usage counts are only collected if you opt in. You can export or delete everything. See the privacy notice.

Can I import my syllabus or PDF?

Paste the text of your syllabus (from a document or a text-based PDF), or use a CSV or .ics file. You check every row before it's saved. Scanned PDFs can't be read yet.

Does it sync with Google Calendar?

Not yet. Today you can import and export .ics files, which work with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and Outlook. Live sync is planned and won't be advertised until it's built and tested.

Can I try it without signing up?

Yes — the demo runs on sample data in your browser and never touches a real account.
