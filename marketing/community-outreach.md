**Title:** I built a free planner that reschedules your work when you fall behind. Looking for honest feedback.

Hi all, I'm a student/developer and I made this because every planner I tried turned into a list of things I'd already failed to do.

**Student Scheduler** does three things:

1. **Turns deadlines into a realistic plan.** You set how much you can actually work per day. It spreads assignments over the days before they're due, around your classes.
2. **Fixes the plan when you miss stuff.** "Catch up" moves missed work to days that still have room and explains why ("due Thursday, 3 h left, Wednesday has 2 h free"). You approve it first; nothing gets deleted.
3. **Reads your syllabus.** Paste the schedule part and it finds assignments, exams and class times. Anything unclear is flagged for you to check.

There's no AI. It's plain rules, so the same input always gives the same plan. It's free during the beta, and there's a demo that works without an account: [link]

I'd really like to hear:

- Does the catch-up suggestion make sense for how you actually work?
- What did the syllabus import get wrong for your course?
- What would make you open this every day?

Thanks! I'll read every reply.
