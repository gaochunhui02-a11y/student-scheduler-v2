# Product description

**One line (≤ 80 chars)**
Your semester, planned automatically, and fixed when life changes.

**Short (≈ 150 chars)**
A student planner that turns your deadlines into a realistic day-by-day plan and rebuilds it, with your OK, when you fall behind.

**Medium (≈ 60 words)**
Student Scheduler plans your semester for you. Add your courses and deadlines, or paste your syllabus, then set how much you can realistically do each day. Big assignments get split across the days before they're due. Missed work gets moved to days that still have room, and the app explains every choice before anything changes. Free during the beta.

**Long**
Most planners make you do the planning. Student Scheduler does the organizing so you can do the work.

- **Set up in minutes.** Paste your syllabus, or import a CSV or .ics file. The app picks out assignments, exams and weekly classes, flags anything unclear, and saves only what you confirm.
- **A plan you can keep.** Choose your usual hours and a realistic daily amount of work. Deadlines are spread over the days before they're due, around your classes, and never over your limit.
- **Recovery built in.** Missed a day? Catch up finds free time before each deadline and explains it: "Due Thursday, 3 h left, Wednesday has 2 h free." Locked sessions never move, nothing is deleted, and you approve every change.
- **"What should I do next?"** The Today page answers it in one card, with your progress and the free time you have left.
- **Everything a student needs.** Courses, grades and final-exam calculators, exam revision, attendance, projects, goals, routines, focus timer, calendar and insights. Available in English, Chinese and Japanese.
- **Private by default.** No ads, no data selling, no AI processing of your content. Usage statistics are opt-in.

Free during the beta. Student Pro (whole-semester planning, advanced forecasting and insights) is planned at $3.99/month or $29/year.
