# Feedback interview guide (25–30 min)

Ask for consent to take notes or record. Ask about past behaviour, not hypotheticals. Don't pitch.

## Warm-up (3 min)
1. What are you studying, and how many courses this term?
2. Walk me through how you kept track of assignments last week.

## Current pain (7 min)
3. Tell me about the last time a deadline snuck up on you. What happened?
4. When you fall behind, what do you actually do? Show me if you can.
5. Which tools have you tried (paper, Notion, Google Calendar, other apps)? Why did you stop using them?

## Using Student Scheduler (10 min)
6. How did setup go? Where did you hesitate?
7. If you imported a syllabus: what did it get wrong? What did you have to fix?
8. Did you use Catch up or Plan my deadlines? Did the explanation make sense? Did you trust the plan?
9. On Today, was the "Do this next" suggestion the right thing? When wasn't it?
10. What did you ignore or never find?

## Value (5 min)
11. If the app disappeared tomorrow, what would you miss, if anything?
12. What would make you open it every day?
13. Have you ever paid for a study or productivity tool? What made it worth it?
14. *(Only at the end)* Pro is planned with whole-semester planning and advanced forecasts, at about $3.99/month or $29/year. What would it need to do for you to consider it? What would feel unfair to put behind a paywall?

## Wrap-up (2 min)
15. Anything I didn't ask that I should have?
16. Can I follow up after the next update?

After each interview, log the top 3 problems, notable quotes, and whether this person used catch-up.
