## How it works
1. **Add your courses and deadlines.** Type them in, or paste your syllabus. Dates, exams and weekly classes are picked out for you to check.
2. **Say how much you can really do.** Pick your usual hours and a realistic amount of work per day. Classes and fixed events are worked around.
3. **Get a day-by-day plan.** Big assignments are split over the days before they're due, so nothing lands on the night before.
4. **Fall behind? It gets fixed.** Missed work is moved to days that still have room. You see exactly where and why before anything changes.

## Feature: Recovery, not guilt
When life happens, "Catch up" finds free time before each deadline and shows its reasoning in plain words. For example: "Due Thursday with 3 h of work left. Wednesday has 2 h free, so it starts there."
- Every change is previewed first; nothing moves until you confirm
- Locked sessions and fixed events never move
- Warns you honestly when there isn't enough time

## Feature: Your syllabus in, minutes not hours
Paste the schedule from your syllabus, or bring a CSV or .ics file. You get an editable table. Anything unclear, like whether 3/4 means March 4 or 3 April, waits for your confirmation.
- Never invents dates or deadlines
- Spots duplicates and past items
- Weekly classes become your timetable
